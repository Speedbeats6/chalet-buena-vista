A Modern Retreat in Nature

Chalet Buena Vista is a beautifully designed haven for up to 4 guests, situated perfectly to offer breathtaking views over Bouillon and the winding Semois river.

While enjoying absolute privacy on your spacious terrace, you also have full access to the vibrant amenities of Holidaypark Papillon, including a swimming pool, cozy bar, and extensive sports facilities.
